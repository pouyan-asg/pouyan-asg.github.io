I'm a robotics developer based in Montréal, Canada, with experience in robotic manipulation, control systems, and automation. I focus on developing and integrating robotic solutions that combine software, hardware, and intelligent control to address real-world challenges. My expertise spans robotics software development, motion planning, system integration, and human-robot interaction, complemented by a background in computer vision and applied machine learning. My work emphasizes bridging the gap between research and practical applications by building reliable, efficient, and user-oriented robotic systems.

Beyond my technical work, I've shared my interest in AI and robotics through *RoboTik*, a Persian-language podcast exploring developments in these fields. I'm also passionate about knowledge sharing, mentorship, and continuous learning, with experience participating in conferences, technical events, and collaborative initiatives. For a more detailed overview of my background and experience, please visit the CV section.
