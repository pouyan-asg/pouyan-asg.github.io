### Introduction

To gain new experiences, I joined an early-stage startup in Montreal, Quebec, called Acrylic Robotics. The core idea behind the startup is to create art using robotics and AI. The artworks are initially designed by digital artists, and after a series of processing steps, they are physically painted by a robotic arm.

When I joined the team in mid-September 2022, the company was in the process of developing its system to produce larger-scale artworks and to achieve a more stable and reliable pipeline for creating different types of acrylic paintings. I worked there as a PhD intern until the end of 2023, during which time we went through many development phases and participated in several events and exhibitions.

During this period, I worked hands-on with various robotic arms, including the Mecademic Meca500, Kinova Gen3 Lite, Kinova Gen3, and Kinova Link 6. In parallel, I was closely involved in AI-related developments that supported the artistic and technical goals of the project. In the following sections, I share selected materials and results from my journey at Acrylic Robotics.
