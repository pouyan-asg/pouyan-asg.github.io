My talks in 2025

January 1, 2026

2026   ·   podcast   Youtube   talk     ·   Lifestyle  

Over the past year, I’ve had the opportunity to take part in several talks and discussions related to AI and robotics. In this post, I’d like to share them, even though some are not very recent. Most of these conversations are in Persian, and the last one is in English.

I was a guest in three episodes of Bidar, a Persian-language podcast based in Canada. In one of these episodes, I talked about the current state of robotics and shared some thoughts and predictions about its future. I really enjoyed this discussion, and I believe the insights shared can be valuable for anyone interested in robotics. I also published the audio version of this talk on my own podcast.

In the second episode, I was invited to talk about Startupfest, a major and popular event held in Montreal. Since I attended the event for two days and had the chance to speak with many participants, I shared my observations, feedback, and personal opinions. The Persian YouTube version of this episode can be found here:

YouTube

The third episode focused on a broader question: Where are we heading in the world of AI? In this conversation, I tried to summarize key ideas from my podcast while adding new perspectives and reflections. Although there are some overlaps with topics I’ve discussed in previous episodes of Robotik, I found this talk particularly engaging. The YouTube video is available at the link below:

Finally, I was a guest on Tay-Talk, where we discussed a wide range of topics, from lifestyle to robotics and AI. This episode was more personal, blending my own story with professional experiences. I really enjoyed the conversation with Taymeyah (the host), and I think it turned into a thoughtful and enjoyable episode. The English audio version is available here:
